Welcome, Operator

This is a simulated fire station control room. A new incident is recorded, its severity and location are noted, a free vehicle and a crew are assigned, and the dispatch status is tracked until the incident is closed.

Real emergency? Call 101 (Fire) or 112 (All emergencies). This website is only a college project.

How the System Works

  1. A call comes in and the details are filled in under Incident Intake.
  2. The severity of the incident is decided (Low, Medium, High or Critical).
  3. The operator checks which vehicles are free under Vehicle Availability.
  4. A crew is chosen under Crew Assignment.
  5. The vehicle and crew are sent from Dispatch Status.
  6. After the fire is put out, the incident is closed and saved in Incident History.
